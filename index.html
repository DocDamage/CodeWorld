Explore Briarwick

Overlook Drag to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

Walk Move with WASD, the arrow keys, or the on-screen arrows. Drag to look. Press E or tap the prompt at doors, the armory sluice, the drawbridge winches, or Aurelith’s vault.

Alder Lake Find the jetty in Places or walk south from the village. Select a rod, binoculars, or stone with 1–3. Press F or tap the action to cast, raise the binoculars, or throw. A low throw skips farther. Hold a creature in your binoculars to record a sighting.

Playing a fish Set the hook when the float dips. Hold F, X / □, or the reel button to wind in. Use [ and ] to loosen or tighten drag, or D-pad left and right during a fight. Ease the drag to let a running fish take line. Tighten it as the fish tires to gain line. A straining line can snap. Too much slack lets the fish shake the hook.

Woodland hunting Find 20 species across four forest habitats. Select binoculars with 1 or the hunting rifle with 2. B raises or lowers the optics. F fires the equipped rifle. Walk up to downed game and press E to collect it. On a controller, bumpers switch equipment, D-pad up toggles optics, and X / □ fires.

Sailing Board or dock with E at the jetty. Steer with A and D. Under sail, W sheets in and S eases out. Sail across the wind or tack to make headway upwind. With the motor or oars, W and S drive forward or astern. Drop anchor to fish. On a controller, use the bumpers for equipment, X / □ to use it, and D-pad right for propulsion and D-pad down for the anchor.

Weather Let weather cycle through the valley or select a condition. Rain raises the brook and speeds up the armory wheel.

Farm work Use the action prompt at field signs, troughs, and nests. Water planted rows from the can or let rain soak them. Refill at the tower. At the tractor, use the movement controls to drive. Press E or A / × for the plow, R or X / □ to hitch the wagon, and Escape or B / ○ to dismount.

Controller Use the left stick to move, right stick to look, and press the left stick to sprint. A / × interacts, B / ○ goes back, and Y / △ toggles Walk. Use the D-pad in menus, triggers to zoom in Overlook, View for Places, and Menu / Options for settings.

Tour Choose Walk here, press Escape, or use B / ○ to take control at the current stop. Open-water stops put you aboard the sailboat. Choose Resume tour to return to the same moment and speed. Adjust Tour speed with [ and ] or the controller bumpers. Choose Restart tour in Places to return to the first shot.

Ore railway The mine opens off the Nameless Sanctum at the bottom of the Briar Crypts. Use E or the action prompt at a vein with the cart nearby. Board the cart and drive forward to bring the load to the blacksmith, or reverse to go back underground. Dismount at the yard and unload. Your ore goes into storage at Brook Armory. Inside, press E at the hearth or anvil to smelt bars and forge tools. Crafted tools equip automatically.

Shortcuts Press H to hide the controls. Escape steps outside from an interior or returns to the overlook from the village.
