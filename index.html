Overlook Drag to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

Walk Move with WASD, the arrow keys, or the on-screen arrows. Drag the view to look around. Walk up to a front door and press E or tap its name to enter. The stairs lead to the loft. Tour Follow the village from daylight to lantern light. Choose a place to visit it directly.

Shortcuts Press H to hide the controls. Escape steps outside from an interior or returns to the overlook from the village.
